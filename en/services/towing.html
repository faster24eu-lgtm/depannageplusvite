Towing

When the car can't get itself anywhere, it's towed to a garage of your choice, or somewhere we help you find if you don't already have one in mind.

Call Now WhatsApp

When towing is the right call

Mechanical failure, a jammed gearbox, an engine that's given out, or a flat with no usable spare: in these cases it's better not to push on and risk making things worse, or to end up stranded in a live lane.

Where the car goes

Already have a garage or dealership in mind? We'll take it straight there. Don't have one? We can point you towards one in the area.

Stuck on a fast road or busy junction? Hazard lights, hi-vis vest, get somewhere safe first. See our périphérique breakdown guide (in French) for the detailed steps.
