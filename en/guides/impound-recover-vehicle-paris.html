Impounded Vehicle in Paris: How to Get It Back

This isn't a breakdown, it's an administrative process. Here's how to find out where your vehicle is, what to bring, and why it's best not to wait.

First, we don't run the impound

Impounding a vehicle and releasing it are handled through an administrative process run by the préfecture de police or the local town hall, not by a private breakdown company. We can't locate your vehicle in their system or speed up its release. What we can do is transport it once it's been returned to you, if you need that.

How to find out where your vehicle is

If your car has disappeared from where you parked it, first check it hasn't simply been moved for roadworks or an event. If not, contact your local police station or the relevant préfecture de police department: they can confirm whether it was towed and tell you which impound it was taken to.

Documents to bring

  • The vehicle's registration certificate (carte grise).
  • A valid driving licence.
  • Proof of ID.
  • A current proof of insurance.

If someone other than the registered keeper is collecting the vehicle, they'll usually need written authorisation and a copy of the owner's ID. Impound and storage fees are set by official order and paid on site; we don't quote figures on this site, as we don't set them.

Why it's best not to wait

Legal time limits apply to impounded vehicles. After a certain point without being claimed, a vehicle can be considered abandoned and be sold or destroyed. The exact time limit depends on your situation and the nature of the offence: check with the impound concerned as soon as possible rather than relying on a rough estimate.

Got your vehicle back? If it won't start or can't be driven away, we can transport it to a garage or an address of your choice. See our vehicle transport page.
