Terms and conditions

The rules that apply to using this site and the connection service.

Last updated: 18 September 2026. The French version prevails if the two differ.

1. Purpose

These terms govern the use of the website depannageplusvite.fr and the connection service offered by Dépannage Plus Vite. By using the site or requesting a job, you accept them.

2. The service

Dépannage Plus Vite connects drivers with a network of vetted breakdown and towing drivers in Paris and the Île-de-France region. The jobs offered are roadside assistance, towing, accident recovery, help with blocked parking or underground car parks, and vehicle transport.

Jobs are carried out by the network's drivers. Releasing a vehicle from a municipal pound is handled by the pound itself: we do not manage that administrative process, but we can transport the vehicle once it has been released to you.

3. Area and availability

The service covers Paris and the Île-de-France region, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. In urgent cases, the target is to be with you within 30 minutes; call us to confirm for your area.

4. Requesting a job

You can reach us by phone, by WhatsApp, or with the "Quick request" form, which prepares a WhatsApp message. To arrange the job, give your location, vehicle type and the nature of the problem accurately.

Your safety comes first: if there is danger or anyone is hurt, call 112 first. The advice published on this site is for information only and does not replace the emergency services or professional advice.

5. Prices

This site publishes no prices. Any question about the price of a job is settled directly with the provider concerned.
